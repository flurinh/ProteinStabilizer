My assessment

Use v2 now for broad screening, not for believing a GPCR decimal.

The 600M hierarchy combines mutation direction, ordered local and global ESM-C context, membrane priors, and learned ProteinMPNN features. It passed every prespecified generic promotion gate and approximately matches the retained 6B baseline. The separate GPCR and membrane transfer heads did not generalize strongly enough to claim calibrated magnitudes, so receptor ranking remains more trustworthy than absolute ΔΔG.

Practical target: rank a broad single-mutant scan, retain general-ΔΔG support, and test a diverse panel rather than only near-duplicate top hits.
