My assessment

Use the consensus to choose GPCR experiments, not to believe a GPCR decimal.

The strict-FP32 6B hierarchy/state fusion is the best signed generic ΔΔG model. GPCR screening adds the retained receptor rank and a small target-excluded GPCRdb family prior. This improves the local GPCR checks, but it remains a within-scan ordering score rather than calibrated stability or crystallization probability.

Practical target: rank a broad single-mutant scan, retain general-ΔΔG support, and test a diverse panel rather than only near-duplicate top hits.
